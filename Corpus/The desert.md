# The desert

A story in progress, from the *stories* sheet. First the plan in six scenes, then the
text sentence by sentence; only the first four sentences are translated so far, and
*xx* marks a word that does not exist yet.

## Plan

1. **The meeting of the three creatures.** A tortoise, a bird with two heads and a fish
   with legs meet at the shore of a vast desert. The tortoise brings a heavy ancient
   stone, the bird a feather, the fish a strange luminous fruit. They stare at each
   other without speaking, as though each knows the others' secret purpose.
2. **The unseen tree.** In the middle of the desert they come upon an invisible tree.
   Each places its object at the base, but nothing happens. They wait as the sun sets,
   their shadows stretching into twisted versions of themselves.
3. **The ascension of the shadows.** Not the creatures move, but their shadows: they
   climb the invisible tree and swirl around its unseen branches, looking down on their
   creators, who stay still.
4. **The judgment of the shadow-fruit.** The shadows come back carrying a fruit from the
   tree and offer it to their creatures. The creatures refuse and look up at the sky,
   where the stars begin to rearrange themselves.
5. **The reversal.** The fish walks into the sky, the bird buries itself in the earth,
   the tortoise dives into the horizon. The untouched fruit begins to glow, floating
   above the ground. No creature or shadow is left to see it.
6. **The stillness of the desert.** The desert, unchanged, except for the floating fruit
   and the glow of the rearranged stars.
