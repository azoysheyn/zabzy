# The song of the birds

Each line: English, a word-for-word gloss, the proto-language where there is one, Zabzy 1.

**1.** The song of the birds
- *This bird-poss. song*
- *xé vézavá nu véfasó*
- **Ki vizavanu vifaso**

**2.** echoed through the morning mist,
- *echo-past-3p. through morning-poss. mist/fog*
- **vunrug ko kugis fa gafanu tysynfy**

**3.** filling the air with melodies
- *xuvún fill-past-3p. ma air with vúnidúso:*
- **kuvun zavinrug ma psan vunduso zu:**

**4.** that seemed to tell stories of places far beyond the horizon.
- *that told seemingly ko rapso about far-beyond-the-horizon-places*
- **kuvun ki vunduso rafynrug sozyn ko rapso kaga dapsav**

Words: [[ki]], [[vizava]], [[nu (possessive)]], [[vifaso]], [[vung]], [[ko]], [[kugis]],
[[fa]], [[gafan]], [[tysynfy]], [[kuvun]], [[zaving]], [[ma]], [[psan]], [[vunduso]],
[[zu]], [[rafyng]], [[sozyn]], [[rapso]], [[kaga]], [[dapsav]].

Line 2 says *echoed* as *vunrug ko kugis* — *did an echo*, with [[vung]] *to do*.
