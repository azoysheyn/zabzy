# The wise elder

> The wise elder, respected by all in the community, has shared stories of the past and
> will continue to guide the young.

Each line: English, a word-for-word gloss, the proto-language, Zabzy 1.

**1.** The wise elder, respected by all in the community,
- *everyone respected him, wise-old person*
- *mazánex-mazánex xéfulánelúgi ma gi dázi: gexél nu xut nu závizí*
- **mazmazang kifuranrug ma daz: kir-kutnu zabzy**

**2.** has shared stories of the past
- *tells old stories towards young persons*
- *lafínelúgi xo xut nú lafisó ma vaxímex nu závizí*
- **rafynrug ko kutnu rapso ma vakynu zabzy**

**3.** and will continue to guide the young.
- *and go-to guide towards them*
- *xuvun latúnelú ta sávizínelú líxetú ma dázi.*
- **kuvun ratunta sabzynru ryktu ma daz.**

Words: [[mzang (many)]], [[kifurang]], [[ma]], [[daz]], [[kirnu]], [[kutnu]], [[zabzy]],
[[rafyng]], [[ko]], [[rapso]], [[vakynu]], [[kuvun]], [[ratung]], [[-ta]], [[sabzyng]],
[[ryktu]]. Future construction: [[Verbs]].

The proto lines spell *ɬ* as *l*, where the lexicon has *r*: *xéfulánelúgi* for
*xefuránex*, *lafínelúgi* for *rafínex*.
