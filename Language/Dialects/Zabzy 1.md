# Zabzy 1

The main dialect, and the only one with a written history: runic, then book hand, then
literary. Every lexeme note is named after its Zabzy 1 form.

- Retroflex series: *t d n r s z* are ʈ ɖ ɳ ɻ ʂ ʐ.
- Old *ʙ ʙ̥* became *bz*, *ps* — hence the name, *zabzy* from *zavisí*.
- Person and tense on the verb: [[Verbs]].
- Two scripts: [[Print]] and [[Handwriting]].
