# Zabzy 2

- Long vowels became diphthongs: *í → ía*, *é → éj*, *á → áj*; *ó → ö*, *ú → ü*.
- Short *ă* reduced to *e* [ə].
- Keeps *ɸ β* and the trills *ʙ ʙ̥* (written *pf*, *bv*).
- **The verb does not change for person**: [[Verbs]].
- A linking vowel before the adjective marker: *kéj-r-e-nu*: [[Adjectives]].
- About a quarter of the lexicon has no Zabzy 2 form yet.
