# Zabzy 3

- Old short vowels left a **low tone**, written with a grave accent: *làsuń*.
- *ɸ β* became *xw w*; *θ ð* became *s z*; *x ɣ* became *c j* [ç ʝ].
- *ŋ* became *ń* [ɲ].
- **Adjectives merged with nouns**: *jel* is both *wisdom* and *wise*: [[Adjectives]].
- Singular pronouns and *many* are borrowings: *co*, *śta*, *luź*, *źulsàn*: [[Pronouns]].
