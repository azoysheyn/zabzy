# Adjectives

An adjective is a noun plus the marker [[nu (adjective)]]: [[kir]] *wisdom* → [[kirnu]]
*wise*, [[kut]] *stone* → [[kutnu]] *old*, [[fy (water)]] → [[fynu]] *watery*.

| proto | Zabzy 1 | Zabzy 2 | Zabzy 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *gexér nu* | *kirnu* | *kéjr-e-nu* | *jel* |
| *xut nu* | *kutnu* | *küt-u-nu* | *cus* |
| *lóvef nu* | *rofnu* | *röf-a-nu* | *low* |

Zabzy 2 puts a linking vowel before *-nu*. Zabzy 3 has merged adjectives with nouns:
*jel* is both *wisdom* and *wise*.

Two adjectives can form a fixed pair. The first one drops *-nu*: *kir-kutnu* *wise-old*,
from [[kirnu]] + [[kutnu]].

Adverbs are marked with [[my]].
