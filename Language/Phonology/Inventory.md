## Vowels

Proto has short and long vowels. The short *ĭ* and *ĕ* were lost before writing appeared.

| proto | Zabzy 1 | Zabzy 2 | Zabzy 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ĭ | — | — | — |
| í | y [ɨ] | ía [iːə] | i [ə] |
| ĕ | — | — | — |
| é | i | éj [eːɪ] | e [ɛ] |
| ă | a | e [ə] | à [æ], low tone |
| á | a | áj [aːɪ] | a [æ] |
| ŏ | o | a | ò [ø], low tone |
| ó | o | ö [ø] | o [ø] |
| ŭ | u | u | ù [y], low tone |
| ú | u | ü | u [y] |

In Zabzy 3 the old short vowels left a low tone, written with a grave accent.
